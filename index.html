About PolyWorkBench

PolyWorkBench evaluates LLM agents on complex, multilingual, long-horizon tasks requiring multi-step tool use, cross-lingual understanding, and structured output generation.

Evaluation Tracks:

  • Grade — Task-specific structural scoring; reported as Pass@1 (best-run mean over 67 tasks) and Pass@3 (per-task best-of-three), the primary ranking metrics
  • Pytest — Executable pass/fail verification of on-disk artefacts (diagnostic)
  • Judge — LLM-as-Judge semantic quality assessment (diagnostic)

Current Status: 32 model×harness entries (8 base models × 4 harnesses: ClaudeCode, OpenClaw, Hermes, Codex), up to 3 completed runs per entry. Domain-level Grade follows the paper's heatmap; Judge is shown where reported in the paper; cells marked “—” are not yet published.
